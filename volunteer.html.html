The 10 designated service roles unlocking as the RV Ministry deploys and membership grows.

On-the-Ground RV Revival Crew 5 Street Roles

1. Advance City Liaison / Permitting Scout

Coordinates with municipal offices ahead of the RV's arrival to secure lawful permits, verify encampment proximity, and rent authorized spaces so the 30-day ministry revival remains fully compliant with local law.

2. Revival Setup & Teardown Crew

Assists with the physical setup of outdoor revival gathering spaces, weather canopies, tables, and sound equipment, and provides thorough site cleanup when the 30-day residency concludes.

3. Food Preparation & Meal Team

Prepares and serves warm, nourishing meals directly to brothers and sisters living within and around the encampments during the revival.

4. Gear & Supply Distributors

Organizes and distributes critical physical survival provisions including cold-weather sleeping bags, weatherproof tents, work boots, everyday shoes, heavy sweaters, and hygiene items.

5. Voucher & Store Transportation Drivers

Provides verified store vouchers and dedicated, respectful round-trip transportation so encampment members can shop for their own essential provisions at local stores with total dignity. (Note: Vehicle mechanical upkeep is handled under commercial sponsorship contracts.)

Remote, Pastoral & Church Defense 5 Support Roles

6. Crisis Line Volunteers

Answers urgent church support communications for individuals navigating acute personal crises, emotional trauma, or life-safety emergencies.

7. Volunteer to be a Prayer Warrior

Dedicated personal intercession standing in the gap for the physical and spiritual needs posted by the flock, preserving the church standard that only living human hearts pray.

8. Drug & Addiction Counselors

Experienced and lived-experience recovery mentors providing guidance, sobriety support, and pathways to medical detox and long-term recovery beds.

9. Volunteer Lawyers for the Church

Licensed legal counsel defending and safeguarding God's Church for the Homeless itself—protecting ministry rights against hostile city ordinances, handling municipal disputes, and defending street outreach operations.

10. Clerical & Ministry Bookkeepers

Assists with managing church administrative records, voucher distribution ledgers, and operational documentation with integrity and order.
