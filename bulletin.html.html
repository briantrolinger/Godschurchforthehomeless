Pastoral Notice

Servant Leader Health & Emergency Communication Protocol

In this church, Brother Brian will never keep the flock in the dark. If Brother Brian is sick, in the hospital, or facing severe trials, an urgent alert will be posted here immediately so the body can pray with open eyes.


As soon as Brother Brian is healed, released from medical care, or the trial is resolved, that notice is removed so old news never lingers on this board. What is posted here will always reflect the current, living truth.
