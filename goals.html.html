🕊️ The Ground-Up Calling & The Last Sheep

God’s Church for the Homeless is brand new. We are literally starting this church from the sidewalk up, building stone by stone. This ministry is not founded on corporate real estate or comfortable traditions; it is founded solely on answering the direct, urgent calling from God to go out into the highways, under the bridges, and along the cold curbs to bring the last lost sheep home to Him.

A Truthful Word From Servant Leader Brother Brian Keith Trolinger

"I want to be 100% honest and transparent with everyone who steps foot into this church. In my personal records and bio, I carry my biblical certificates. But I am not called a pastor, and I do not claim titles that are not mine—that is why I go simply by Brother Brian.

I am currently working diligently to complete my official legal ordainment so that our church and every single one of our members are 100% legally recognized and protected under the law. But let me be entirely clear: a man does not need a man-made diploma or a college degree to preach the uncompromised Word of the Living God. It is only the civil government that requires these legal credentials to recognize specific pastoral rights. We fulfill this requirement so Caesar has no hold over the ministry, while our eyes remain fixed on Christ alone."

Every step forward will be taken with full integrity, open eyes, and unwavering obedience to the Lord Jesus Christ.

🚐 The Street RV Ministry Objectives

The heart of this church's physical deployment is taking the sanctuary directly to where the broken live. Below are the foundational objectives established specifically for the RV Outreach:

  • ⭐
    30-Day Encampment Revivals

    Deploying the flagship RV directly alongside forgotten homeless encampments for a minimum of 30 days at a time to preach, counsel, and break bread together.

  • ⭐
    Advance Permitting & Legal Placement

    Sending advance liaisons into each municipality ahead of arrival to secure authorized lot rentals and city permits, keeping the ministry 100% compliant with local law.

  • ⭐
    Physical Survival & Essential Distribution

    Providing direct on-site relief including winter sleeping bags, thermal wear, boots, and clean socks to protect lives during extreme seasons.

  • ⭐
    Dignity Vouchers & Store Transportation

    Issuing essential store vouchers to street brothers and sisters, accompanied by dedicated rides to local stores so they can shop with dignity.
