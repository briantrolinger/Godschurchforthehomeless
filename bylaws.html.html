Official Mission Statement

"Church for the Homeless exists to save lives, save souls, and help heal the broken by bringing the love of Jesus directly to the lost and unhoused on American streets. In a society that often criminalizes poverty and walks past the hungry, we believe that everyone has a story, everyone deserves to be loved, and everyone deserves to be treated like a human being rather than a nuisance."

"We do not require the hurting to come to a building; instead, we look in our own backyard, going directly to the fields of people who need to know that their current situation is not the end of their story. Our mission is to meet people exactly where they are—whether they are rich or poor, if you don't know Jesus you are Homeless—look them in the eye with God-given compassion, and help them accept the love, redemption, and hope that can only come through Jesus Christ."

The Three Core Beliefs

1. The Priority of the Seed

"We believe that the planting of seeds of hope and the Gospel is the single most important work we do. We are not called to force an immediate harvest or judge a person's timeline for recovery; we are called to faithfully plant the seed of Jesus Christ in broken soil, just as someone once did for us, and trust God to make it grow."

2. Open Eyes and Compassionate Hearts

"We believe that true ministry requires eyes that have been opened by God to see the invisible. Compassion for our fellow man is not optional. We believe in taking the time to see, care for, and genuinely listen to those the rest of the world walks by."

3. Redemption of Pain

"We believe that Jesus Christ rescues people from the darkest pits of life—hunger, abuse, trauma, and homelessness—and transforms those devastating experiences into the very tools He uses to reach others. Our pain becomes our purpose."

Our Vision for America

"Our vision is to revive the spirit of the Good Samaritan on American streets, sparking a movement that helps our country become a nation of compassion and love once again. We envision a ministry that reaches into the darkest places to save as many people from themselves and their circumstances as possible by faithfully planting the seeds of Jesus Christ."

"Our goal is not just to offer temporary relief, but to facilitate lasting transformation—making true disciples who turn their entire lives over to Jesus. Ultimately, we see a future where the Church for the Homeless expands beyond a single outreach into a nationwide fleet of mobile RVs, led by pastors who have lived the struggle, actively planting seeds and making disciples everywhere they go."

The Five Core Values

• Radical Hospitality

We believe in creating a sanctuary where every individual, regardless of their housing status, is welcomed with the dignity and respect they deserve as children of God.

• Unconditional Compassion

Our mission is rooted in meeting people where they are, offering grace without judgment and support without strings attached.

• Communal Healing

We foster a family environment that breaks the cycle of isolation, recognizing that spiritual and emotional restoration happens best within a supportive community.

• Sacred Advocacy

Beyond immediate relief, we are committed to speaking truth to power and seeking justice for those the world has overlooked.

• Holistic Stewardship

We honor the physical, mental, and spiritual needs of our neighbors, providing a space where the soul can find rest while the body finds nourishment.

History of the Church for the Homeless

"The vision for the Church for the Homeless was not born in a classroom or a boardroom; it was forged in the reality of the streets. It is a new and radical response to a broken system. My own life was marked by trauma, years of homelessness, and a desperate struggle to find hope. I know what it feels like to be invisible, to be ignored, and to be treated as less than human. I have seen firsthand how society—and even the church—often turns away from the dirty, the hungry, and the forgotten. I can no longer stand by and watch."

"This ministry is funded by the truth of my own experience and the grace that pulled me out of it. I have lived the pain, and I have found the power of Jesus Christ to transform a life. My goal is simple: to share that same love with every lost soul in America. We aren't here to judge; we are here to walk alongside."

"We are launching this church because America needs to remember what it means to love its people—the rich and the poor, the housed and the unhoused—with equal dignity. We believe that if you give someone a chance, remind them they are a precious child of God, and show them that someone cares, you can change their life."

"This mission is as vital as the air we breathe. We aren't waiting for people to walk through a church door; we are bringing the Church to where the people are. If this ministry reaches just one person—if it helps just one individual rediscover their worth in Jesus—then we have succeeded. We are here to bring the Gospel back to the streets, to be a voice for those who have been silenced, and to prove that no one is ever too far gone to be loved."

Pastoral Qualifications & Protections (The Bylaws)

Because the nature of this ministry requires an unparalleled depth of compassion and a profound connection to the people we serve, the leadership of this church operates under a unique standard of lived experience and grace:

Section 1. The Lived Experience Requirement

"To be ordained, hired, or appointed as a pastor for Church for the Homeless, an individual must have personal, lived experience with deep hardship. They must have either survived homelessness themselves or endured a terrible life experience from which God rescued them. A pastor in this ministry cannot look at the unhoused and say, 'I do not understand them.' You have to have lived this to understand this, to truly see the hurt, and to possess a genuine willingness to care and listen. Academic theology is not a substitute for a survival testimony."

Section 2. Protection from Disqualification by Divorce

"We recognize that human brokenness touches every life, and that God's grace covers the deep pain of a broken marriage. Therefore, no pastor, assistant pastor, or ministry leader within Church for the Homeless shall be fired, removed from office, or disqualified from leadership on the basis of a past or present divorce. Our leadership is defined by a person's current walk with Christ and their relentless heart for the lost, not by marital history."
