Notice to all visitors and supporters: We are currently not accepting online credit card donations, checks, or financial gifts of any kind on this website.

📖 Our Stand of Integrity

God's Church for the Homeless is a purely digital church right now. Just like anything online, when our giving door eventually opens, it will operate cleanly through secure credit card processing. There is no physical headquarters, no shipping warehouse, and no street address. One day maybe we will have that ground, but we are not there yet.

This church is brand new and still in its infant stage. Fulfilling official tax exemptions and securing proper non-profit legal status takes both time and money. Up to this very hour, my wife and I have personally been putting everything we own into getting this church off the ground out of our own pockets.

Unfortunately, I cannot ask you for help in this matter right now. Until we are legally established, I cannot even open a dedicated church bank account. And I am simply not willing to compromise this church by cutting corners just to take a donation. I will not accept money into a personal bank account, and I will not bend the rules.

"Getting the Word of God out to the lost sheep is far more important to me than any donation I could ever receive. I will wait and do it right. God deserves His home built right."

The second our tax-exempt standing is sealed, our fiscal covering is in place, and our official church bank account is opened, we will announce it immediately on the Community Bulletin Board so all members and supporters know the door is open. Until then, we stand in faith, obedience, and total legal honesty.

Brother Brian Keith Trolinger Servant Leader | God's Church for the Homeless
